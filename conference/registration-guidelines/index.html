Registration Guidelines

02.

03.

Registration will be confirmed only after the payment process is completed.

04.

International participants requiring visa support may indicate this within the registration form.

Payment Information

The Secretariat will share official bank details and instructions after registration.

Official invoices and payment confirmations will be issued by the Secretariat of ICFIRE 2026.

Registration & Cancellation Policy

All cancellations of registration should be notified in writing via email to: info@amber-laboratory.com

Cancellation refunds will be processed as follows:

  • Before 30th June 2026: Full refund minus a 25% administrative fee
  • On or after 30th June 2026: No refund will be issued

Refunds will be processed within 30 working days after the conference.

Substitution Policy

Registered participants who are unable to attend may nominate a substitute at no additional cost, provided they give written notice.

Travel & Accommodation Information

For International Participants

The Secretariat of ICFIRE 2026 warmly welcomes international delegates to Colombo, Sri Lanka. To support your travel planning, the following information is provided for guidance.

Travel to Sri Lanka

Participants requiring an official invitation letter for visa purposes may request one during the registration process.

Arrival in Colombo

ICFIRE 2026 will be held at:
BMICH (Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall) Colombo, Sri Lanka

The nearest international airport is:
Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB)

Accommodation

Colombo offers a wide range of accommodation options to suit all preferences and budgets, from star hotels in the city centre to serviced apartments and charming guest houses, including:

  • 5-Star Hotels
  • 4-3 Star Business Hotels
  • Boutique Hotels
  • Serviced Apartments

Comfortable, well-connected, and close to the sea, these options reflect the spirit of Colombo: authentic, welcoming, and easy to enjoy, even on a tight schedule. All you have to do is choose what works best for your stay

Local Transportation

Transportation options in Colombo include:

  • Ride-hailing services (Uber, PickMe)
  • Hotel taxis
  • Private car rentals

Travel time from most central Colombo hotels to BMICH ranges from 10 to 20 minutes.
