Call for Images

International Conference on Fire Safety and Resilience of the Built Environment - ICFIRE 2026

March 3, 2026 – Beginning of submission for images
June 15, 2026 – Submission deadline for images
June 30, 2026 – Notification of acceptance of images

Submitting your image

  • Images and captions (no more than 100 words / 500 characters) should be electronically submitted by June 15, 2026, through email: info@amber-laboratory.com
  • The image format should be jpg or png with a maximum image size of 5 Mbyte for submission review.
  • Images submitted after the June 15, 2026, deadline will not be considered.

Image Submission / Review

  • Images: Must be original work, non-commercial, non-promotional, and not AI-generated. For submission through the Amber laboratory website, ICFIRE 2026, or email, the image format should be jpg, png, or bmp, with the maximum image size for submission review of 5 Mbyte.
  • Style: Images may correspond to experimental studies, numerical studies, or actual events related to fire safety science.
  • Caption: A brief figure caption describing the image is expected to accompany the image. Captions are limited to 100 words / 500 characters. The submitter should also include their name and affiliation with the caption, but this information is not included in the word count.
  • Digital Display: Submitters will be provided a display that will include their image, caption, name, and affiliation. Submitters are not required to be in attendance at their image during the display session.

Example

  • Title: Fire Resistance Test of Concrete Column
  • Caption: This image shows a reinforced concrete column undergoing a fire resistance test in a furnace to evaluate structural stability under high temperatures.
  • Author: Dr. Jane Smith
  • Affiliation: University of Melbourne, Australia
