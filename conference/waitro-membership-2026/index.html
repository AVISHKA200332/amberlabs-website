Amber Laboratory Joins the WAITRO Global Innovation Family

We are pleased to announce that Amber Laboratory has officially joined the World Association of Industrial and Technological Research Organizations (WAITRO) as an Associate Member in 2026.

Global Partnership Associate Member 2026

We are pleased to announce that Amber Laboratory has officially joined the World Association of Industrial and Technological Research Organizations (WAITRO) as an Associate Member in 2026.

This membership marks an important milestone in Amber Laboratory’s journey toward strengthening international collaboration and expanding its engagement with the global research and innovation community.

As part of WAITRO, Amber Laboratory joins a network of more than 190 research and technology organizations, universities, and industries across over 80 countries.

WAITRO’s global network promotes international collaboration, capacity development & knowledge sharing, technology transfer & commercialization, Entrepreneurship & innovation, and Policy & Advocacy.

This new partnership further strengthens Amber Laboratory’s commitment to advancing technology, research, innovation, and sustainable development while connecting local expertise with the global research community.
