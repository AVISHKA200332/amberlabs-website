SERVICES

Materials & Assemblies Testing for Fire Resistance

We operate an advanced, internationally compliant fire-resistance testing facility, providing certification in accordance with recognized international standards. Our ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and ILAC-accredited services deliver reliable, globally accepted test data to support regulatory compliance, product quality, and safety requirements for the built environment.

Our Tests include:

  • Penetration Seals – Pipes, Wraps, Collars, Cables, Conduits, Bus Bars, Mix Penetrations
  • Fire Dampers
  • Linear Joint Seals
  • Smoke Control Dampers
  • Cable Systems and Associated Components
  • Fire Door and Shutter Assemblies
  • Openable Windows
  • Building Hardware Elements
  • Smoke Control Test for Door and Shutter Assemblies
  • Non-loadbearing Elements - Walls, Curtain Walling, Air Transfer Grilles
  • Tunnel linings

Importance of These Tests:

  • Ensure effective fire compartmentation
  • Protect designated escape routes
  • Prevent the spread of fire and smoke between spaces
  • Support compliance with building codes and regulations
  • Improve life safety and safeguard property

Research, Development, and Innovation

We provide specialised research and innovation services to support the development, optimisation, and validation of fire-resistance materials, systems, and assemblies.

We collaborate with researchers, manufacturers, and designers, and generate reliable technical evidence that enables innovation while ensuring compliance with international fire safety standards.

Our R&D activities focus on fire-resistant construction materials, including high-performance composites, protective coatings, and bio-based and recycled solutions. We also conduct performance evaluations of new and modified products, develop tailored test methods for non-standard applications, and support continuous product improvement. Through advanced fire testing and data-driven analysis, we help transform innovative ideas into compliant, market-ready fire safety solutions.
